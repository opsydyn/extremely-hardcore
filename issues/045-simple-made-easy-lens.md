# issue: Simple Made Easy lens

## intent

Add a focused simplicity lens to EHS while keeping EHS itself as the product,
package, and engineering method.

## scope

- Add the optional `simple-made-easy` skill.
- Route it from `using-extremely-hardcore` for multi-option or
  boundary-sensitive changes.
- Use a YAGNI/reuse ladder and structural anti-complecting questions.
- Keep Socratic questions decision-bearing and record settled results in
  existing EHS documents.
- Validate and dogfood the new public skill surface.

## completion criteria

- The validator requires and accepts the new skill.
- The skill clearly distinguishes simple from easy and complete from short.
- Existing safety, evidence, architecture, domain, and test obligations remain
  explicit.
- README, manifesto, user guide, and roadmap explain how the lens composes.
- One real EHS dogfood record captures the multi-option design choice and
  verification evidence.

## non-goals

- No EHS/package rename.
- No new tracker, report, score, protocol schema, runtime command, scheduler,
  daemon, or provider integration.
- No mandatory transcript or simplicity artifact for every change.
